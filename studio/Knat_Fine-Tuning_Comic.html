🔁 THE CYCLE CONTINUES! Not good enough yet? Knat hops back to Panel 3 or 7, tweaks the config or hyperparameters, and runs the whole loop again — that's the "tuning" in fine-tuning.

🧠 Remember it with: F.I.N.E. T.U.N.E.

The 8 core panels spell out the very word this notebook is all about. Say it out loud before you open Colab.

F
Fire up
Panel 1 — check GPU, install libraries.
I
Identify
Panel 2 — log in to Hugging Face / W&B.
N
Name the config
Panel 3 — model, dataset, output dir, seq length.
E
Extract data
Panel 4 — load + format the dataset's "text" field.
T
Transform in 4-bit
Panel 5 — load tokenizer + QLoRA base model.
U
Upgrade w/ LoRA
Panel 6 — freeze base, attach adapter patches.
N
Nail the knobs
Panel 7 — learning rate, epochs, batch, schedule.
E
Engage!
Panel 8 — trainer.train() actually runs.
Then comes S.T.S. — Save, Test, Sweep (Panels 9-11) — and if the sweep says "try again," you loop back to Name-the-config or Nail-the-knobs and re-run the cycle. 🔁

🦟 Meet the Cast & Crew

KNAT The mascot. A gnat-shaped robot who represents the whole notebook — small, fast, and surprisingly good at squeezing into tight GPU memory.
THE BIG FROZEN MODEL Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, loaded in 4-bit. Doesn't move (frozen weights) — Knat's LoRA patches do all the learning.
THE ADAPTER PATCHES The small LoRA matrices (r=16) that get trained. Tiny in file size, big in effect.
THE DATA HOPPER The guanaco-llama2-1k dataset, formatted into one clean "text" field before training.
THE TREADMILL SFTTrainer — where 3 epochs of actual training happen, logging loss the whole way.
THE HUB Hugging Face Hub — optional final destination for the finished adapter.
